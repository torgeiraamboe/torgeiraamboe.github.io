Autonomy

Last year I made a pivot away from pure mathematics. After the pivot I have gone through a sort of identity crisis, as I think a lot of mathematicians going through the same process do. I still work in mathematics, only now more on the applied side. I have not been sure how to continue writing and clearing my thoughts on this blog, as it used to center around my journey through algebraic topology and pure mathematics. As it turns out — which I of course knew — there are a plethora of highly interesting problems and topics to write about. The main reason I haven’t is because I haven’t felt competent enough. But, I think I have decided to power through, and continue writing about more general things that will probably be wildly different from what it used to.
